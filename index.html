Our story

Amai means mother

That is exactly who makes everything you see here. Every candle is poured by hand with natural soy wax, and every crochet piece is worked stitch by stitch at home, with the same care a mother gives to anything made for her family. No two pieces are ever quite the same.

If you have a colour, scent or occasion in mind, from a pair of earrings for a friend to coasters for a new home, we would love to make it for you.

With love, Amai
