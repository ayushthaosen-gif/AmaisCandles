About me

Why it's called Amai's

Amai means mother. My mother is the one who taught me the basics, and I never forgot the feeling of making something with my own hands next to her.

Then I became a doctor, and for all my years in service there was simply no time for it. There was always another patient, another shift, and this quietly got put away.

After I retired, I picked it up again. The moment I started, it all came back, and so did so many memories of her. Now I pour candles and crochet at home, and every piece carries a little of what she taught me.

If you'd like something made as a gift or just for yourself, tell me what you have in mind and I'll make it for you.

With love, Amai
